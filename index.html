Three ways to play

Mission · 1 player

Halvard Depot

A weather station high in the mountains, cut off by snow. Take the red keycard, wreck the guidance console in the lab and slip out through the vehicle tunnel.

  • Sneak up the maintenance duct or kick in the front door
  • Guards patrol, hear footsteps, take cover and run for the alarm
  • Easy, Normal or Hard

Versus · 2 players

Split-screen showdown

Two players, one screen, first to ten takedowns. Player two grabs a gamepad, and it's on.

  • Toy Factory: catwalks, a bridge and the only rifle up in the middle
  • Castle and Space Station
  • No second player? Play against up to three computers
  • Side by side or top and bottom

Training · 1 or 2 players

Questions

What do I need?

A computer with a recent Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari, and a keyboard and mouse or a gamepad. The game is about 4 MB and loads in a few seconds.

How do two people play?

Pick Versus or Training with two players. Player one plays on keyboard and mouse, player two on a gamepad (or both on gamepads). Press a button on the controller to join. Split-screen is side by side or top and bottom, in Settings.

Does it work on phones and tablets?

Not yet. There are no touch controls, so you need a computer.

Is it OK for kids?

It was made to play with kids: toy figures that tip over, no blood, and an Easy setting with slow, forgiving guards.

Does it cost anything? Do I need an account?

No and no. Tin Fox is free. Settings and best times are kept in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere.

Who made it?

Tin Fox is made by kimiyu. Characters, blasters and props are Kenney's free CC0 kits; the font is Fredoka (SIL Open Font License). Levels, textures, sounds and music are made in code.
