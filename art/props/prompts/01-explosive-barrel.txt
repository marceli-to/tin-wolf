Prop reference sheet of one original game item, made to match the art style of the
attached character and weapon sheets exactly. It belongs to the same stylised 3D arena
shooter with a bright, colourful look. The props are retro-futuristic sci-fi objects, not
real-world products.

Style: low-poly 3D game asset, clearly faceted flat-shaded surfaces, flat solid saturated
colours (no textures, no gradients, no grime, no scratches, no dents), chunky simple shapes,
slightly oversized and rounded like the characters, bold readable silhouette.

Item: an explosive barrel, about 90 cm tall and 65 cm across, a hazard players must spot at
once from far away. A chunky upright drum in bright signal red, slightly bulging, with two
raised rims around the body. A wide band of bold yellow and black diagonal hazard stripes around
the middle. On the front and back a big simple yellow warning symbol: a round flame or blast
icon (a symbol only, no letters). A dark grey lid with a stubby valve and a small glowing
yellow-orange pressure light on top. It must read as dangerous and clearly different from the
plain dark grey crates and barrels around it.
Colour palette limited to about 5 colours: signal red, yellow, black, dark grey, white for a
small highlight.

Layout: three views side by side in one row, left to right: FRONT view, SIDE view, TOP view
(looking down on the lid). Strict orthographic views, no perspective, no three-quarter angles.
Same item at the same scale in all views, standing on the same horizontal line, the whole item
visible with space around it. No character, no explosion, no fire, no effects.

Plain flat light grey background (#E8E8E8), no shadow, no floor, no text, no labels, no frames.
