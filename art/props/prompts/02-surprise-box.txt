Prop reference sheet of one original game item, made to match the art style of the
attached character and weapon sheets exactly. It belongs to the same stylised 3D arena
shooter with a bright, colourful look. The props are retro-futuristic sci-fi objects, not
real-world products.

Style: low-poly 3D game asset, clearly faceted flat-shaded surfaces, flat solid saturated
colours (no textures, no gradients, no grime, no scratches, no dents), chunky simple shapes,
slightly oversized and rounded like the characters, bold readable silhouette.

Item: a "surprise box", the game's signature power-up box, an 80 cm cube that players break
open for a random power-up. A friendly, playful, eye-catching cube in bright purple with
rounded edges and chunky gold corner caps and a gold frame around every face. On each of the
four sides and on top a big raised white question mark symbol (the only symbol, no letters or
words). A thin glowing light blue seam just under the lid, as if something inside is shining
through. Instantly recognisable from far away and unlike any other crate in the game.
Colour palette limited to about 5 colours: bright purple, darker purple for the recessed panels,
gold, white, light blue.

Layout: three views side by side in one row, left to right: FRONT view, SIDE view, TOP view.
Strict orthographic views, no perspective, no three-quarter angles. Same item at the same scale
in all views, standing on the same horizontal line, the whole item visible with space around it.
No character, no sparkles, no effects, no opened box.

Plain flat light grey background (#E8E8E8), no shadow, no floor, no text, no labels, no frames.
