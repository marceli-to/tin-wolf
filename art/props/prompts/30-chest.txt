Prop reference sheet of one original game item, made to match the art style of the
attached character, weapon and prop sheets exactly. It belongs to the same stylised 3D
shooter with a bright, colourful look. The props are retro-futuristic sci-fi objects, not
real-world products.

Style: low-poly 3D game asset, clearly faceted flat-shaded surfaces, flat solid saturated
colours (no textures, no gradients, no grime, no scratches, no dents), chunky simple shapes,
slightly oversized and rounded like the characters, bold readable silhouette.

Setting: the Halvard Depot, a snowed-in mountain weather station that the villains turned into a secret
drone lab. Its kit is sturdy, metal, bolted together, in light grey and olive drab with orange
accents and light blue screens.

Item: an equipment chest, 110 cm long, 56 cm high, 59 cm deep, standing on the floor. A
low, sturdy metal footlocker in olive drab with rounded edges, a slightly domed lid, dark grey
corner caps and reinforcement bands, two chunky orange latches and a dark grey carry handle on
each short end. Closed. No letters, no stencils.
Colour palette limited to about 5 colours: olive drab, darker olive for recessed panels, dark grey,
bright orange, black.

Layout: three views side by side in one row, left to right: FRONT view, SIDE view, TOP view.
Strict orthographic views, no perspective, no three-quarter angles. Same item at the same scale
in all views, standing on the same horizontal line, the whole item visible with space around it.
No character, no effects.

Plain flat light grey background (#E8E8E8), no shadow, no floor, no text, no labels, no frames.
