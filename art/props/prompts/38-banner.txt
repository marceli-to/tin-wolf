Prop reference sheet of one original game item, made to match the art style of the
attached character, weapon and prop sheets exactly. It belongs to the same stylised 3D
shooter with a bright, colourful look.

Style: low-poly 3D game asset, clearly faceted flat-shaded surfaces, flat solid saturated
colours (no textures, no gradients, no grime, no scratches, no dents), chunky simple shapes,
slightly oversized and rounded like the characters, bold readable silhouette.

Setting: the Snowfort, an outdoor arena in the snowy mountain yard of the Halvard Depot, a snowed-in
weather station that the villains turned into a secret drone lab. Fresh white snow, dark green pines,
grey granite, and the villains' kit in light grey and olive drab with orange accents.

Item: a tall cloth banner that hangs flat on a wall, 0.75 m wide, 2.6 m tall and very thin (about
5 cm deep including its pole). A short horizontal dark grey metal pole at the top with round end
caps; under it the olive drab banner hangs straight down and ends in a swallowtail (two points).
A thick bright orange border runs down both long sides. In the upper half, a simple bold emblem in
white: a round badge with a stylised mountain peak and a small drone shape over it. No text, no
letters.
Colour palette limited to about 4 colours: olive drab, bright orange, white, dark grey.

Layout: three views side by side in one row, left to right: FRONT view, SIDE view, TOP view.
Strict orthographic views, no perspective, no three-quarter angles. Same item at the same scale
in all views, standing on the same horizontal line, the whole item visible with space around it.
No character, no effects.

Plain flat light grey background (#E8E8E8), no shadow, no floor, no text, no labels, no frames.
