Prop reference sheet of one original game item, made to match the art style of the
attached character, weapon and prop sheets exactly. It belongs to the same stylised 3D
shooter with a bright, colourful look. The props are retro-futuristic sci-fi objects, not
real-world products.

Style: low-poly 3D game asset, clearly faceted flat-shaded surfaces, flat solid saturated
colours (no textures, no gradients, no grime, no scratches, no dents), chunky simple shapes,
slightly oversized and rounded like the characters, bold readable silhouette.

Item: a large supply crate, the big brother of the attached supply crate and clearly from the same
family. A chunky box 155 cm wide, 141 cm high, 154 cm deep: tall enough that a standing soldier can
hide behind it. Olive drab body with rounded edges, a raised frame around every face, each face
divided into four recessed panels by a raised cross, chunky dark grey corner caps, two dark grey
lifting brackets on the top edge of the front and back, and the same bright orange stripe around the
box near the top. Flat top.
Colour palette limited to about 4 colours: olive drab, darker olive for the recessed panels, dark
grey, bright orange.

Layout: three views side by side in one row, left to right: FRONT view, SIDE view, TOP view. Strict orthographic views, no
perspective, no three-quarter angles. Same item at the same scale in all views, standing on the
same horizontal line, the whole item visible with space around it. No character, no effects, closed crate.

Plain flat light grey background (#E8E8E8), no shadow, no floor, no text, no labels, no frames.
