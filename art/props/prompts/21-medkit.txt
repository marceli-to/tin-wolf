Pickup reference sheet of one original game item, made to match the art style of the
attached character, weapon and prop sheets exactly. It belongs to the same stylised 3D
shooter with a bright, colourful look. The items are retro-futuristic sci-fi objects, not
real-world products. In the game the item floats and slowly spins above the floor, so it has
to read clearly from every side and from a few metres away.

Style: low-poly 3D game asset, clearly faceted flat-shaded surfaces, flat solid saturated
colours (no textures, no gradients, no grime, no scratches, no dents), chunky simple shapes,
slightly oversized and rounded like the characters, bold readable silhouette.

Item: a medkit, a first-aid case that restores health, about 36 cm wide, 22 cm high,
26 cm deep. A chunky white hard case with rounded edges, a dark grey carry handle on top and
dark grey latches, and on the front and on the back a big raised red heart symbol (the same
heart as a health bar icon; no cross, no letters or words). A red band runs around the case.
Colour palette limited to about 4 colours: white, light grey for the recessed seams, bright red,
dark grey.

Layout: three views side by side in one row, left to right: FRONT view, SIDE view, TOP view.
Strict orthographic views, no perspective, no three-quarter angles. Same item at the same scale
in all views, centred on the same horizontal line, the whole item visible with space around it.
No character, no hands, no effects, no glow ring, no sparkles.

Plain flat light grey background (#E8E8E8), no shadow, no floor, no text, no labels, no frames.
