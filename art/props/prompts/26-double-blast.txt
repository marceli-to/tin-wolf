Power-up reference sheet of one original game item, made to match the art style of the
attached character, weapon and prop sheets exactly. It belongs to the same stylised 3D
arena shooter with a bright, colourful look. Power-ups are the prizes that pop out of the
attached surprise box: playful, cheerful toy objects, a little magical, the most eye-catching
things in the game. In the game the item floats and slowly spins above a glowing ring on the
floor, so it has to read clearly from every side and from far away.

Style: low-poly 3D game asset, clearly faceted flat-shaded surfaces, flat solid saturated
colours (no textures, no gradients, no grime, no scratches), chunky simple shapes, slightly
oversized and rounded like the characters, bold readable silhouette. One strong main colour
per power-up, so players tell them apart by colour alone.

Item: "Double Blast", a double-damage power-up, about 40 cm tall. A chunky cartoon
explosion-star burst with eight thick rounded points in bright red, a yellow star in the
middle and a big raised white "x2" on the front and on the back (the only text allowed, bold and simple).
Loud, punchy, exciting.
Colour palette limited to about 4 colours: bright red, yellow, white, dark red (side faces).

Layout: three views side by side in one row, left to right: FRONT view, SIDE view, TOP view.
Strict orthographic views, no perspective, no three-quarter angles. Same item at the same scale
in all views, centred on the same horizontal line, the whole item visible with space around it.
No character, no hands, no effects, no glow ring, no sparkles.

Plain flat light grey background (#E8E8E8), no shadow, no floor, no text, no labels, no frames.
