Prop reference sheet of one original game item, made to match the art style of the
attached character, weapon and prop sheets exactly. It belongs to the same stylised 3D
shooter with a bright, colourful look. The props are retro-futuristic sci-fi objects, not
real-world products.

Style: low-poly 3D game asset, clearly faceted flat-shaded surfaces, flat solid saturated
colours (no textures, no gradients, no grime, no scratches, no dents), chunky simple shapes,
slightly oversized and rounded like the characters, bold readable silhouette.

Setting: the Halvard Depot, a snowed-in mountain weather station that the villains turned into a secret
drone lab. The furniture is a small military-facility kit: sturdy, metal, bolted together, in
light grey and olive drab with orange accents and light blue screens. Not home furniture, no wood.

Item: the drone guidance console, the prototype machine players must find and destroy, so it must
stand out from every other piece of furniture in the lab. A wide console 140 cm wide, 120 cm high,
70 cm deep. A chunky dark grey base with a slanted desk-style control surface full of chunky buttons
and two lever handles, and on top a big upright housing with a large round light blue radar-style
screen (concentric rings and a sweep line, no letters or numbers) flanked by two small screens. An
orange-and-black hazard frame around the screen, a short antenna with a small glowing orange light on
top, and thick black cables running from the back into the floor.
Colour palette limited to about 6 colours: dark grey, light grey, light blue, bright orange, black,
white for small highlights.

Layout: three views side by side in one row, left to right: FRONT view (screens and controls), SIDE view, TOP view. Strict orthographic views, no
perspective, no three-quarter angles. Same item at the same scale in all views, standing on the
same horizontal line, the whole item visible with space around it. No character, no explosion, no effects.

Plain flat light grey background (#E8E8E8), no shadow, no floor, no text, no labels, no frames.
