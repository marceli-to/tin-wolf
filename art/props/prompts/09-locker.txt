Prop reference sheet of one original game item, made to match the art style of the
attached character, weapon and prop sheets exactly. It belongs to the same stylised 3D
shooter with a bright, colourful look. The props are retro-futuristic sci-fi objects, not
real-world products.

Style: low-poly 3D game asset, clearly faceted flat-shaded surfaces, flat solid saturated
colours (no textures, no gradients, no grime, no scratches, no dents), chunky simple shapes,
slightly oversized and rounded like the characters, bold readable silhouette.

Setting: the Halvard Depot, a snowed-in mountain weather station that the villains turned into a secret
drone lab. The furniture is a small military-facility kit: sturdy, metal, bolted together, in
light grey and olive drab with orange accents and light blue screens. Not home furniture, no wood.

Item: a tall metal locker, 71 cm wide, 192 cm high, 36 cm deep, standing against a wall. Olive drab
body with rounded edges, two tall narrow doors on the front, each with a row of three horizontal vent
slots at the top, a small black handle and an orange number plate (blank, no digits). A dark grey
plinth at the bottom and a dark grey top edge. The back is flat and plain.
Colour palette limited to about 4 colours: olive drab, dark grey, black, bright orange.

Layout: three views side by side in one row, left to right: FRONT view (doors), SIDE view, TOP view. Strict orthographic views, no
perspective, no three-quarter angles. Same item at the same scale in all views, standing on the
same horizontal line, the whole item visible with space around it. No character, no effects, doors closed.

Plain flat light grey background (#E8E8E8), no shadow, no floor, no text, no labels, no frames.
