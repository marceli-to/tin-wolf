Character turnaround sheet of one original game character: a security guard for a
stylised 3D arena shooter with a bright, colourful look.

Style: low-poly 3D game asset, stylised cartoon proportions but not chibi:
clearly faceted flat-shaded surfaces, flat solid saturated colours (no textures,
no gradients, no grime, no scratches), chunky simple shapes, bold readable silhouette.
Proportions: about 5 heads tall, head slightly larger than realistic, broad shoulders,
slightly oversized hands and boots, sturdy legs. Friendly expression with simple
features: a confident, good-natured face with a slight smile (no gore, nothing scary).

Outfit: retro-futuristic security uniform: olive-green jacket with a high collar and
rounded padded shoulder pieces, a chunky black belt with a round brass buckle,
olive trousers tucked into big black boots with thick soles, black gloves, a rounded
olive cap with a short black visor and a small antenna-like badge on the side.
Colour palette limited to about 6 colours: olive green, black, dark grey, skin tone,
brass, and bright orange for accents (shoulder stripes, badge).

Layout: three views side by side in one row, left to right: FRONT view, SIDE view
(character facing left), BACK view. Strict orthographic views, no perspective, no
three-quarter angles. Same character, same size and same height in all three views,
feet resting on the same horizontal line, whole body visible with space around it.
Pose: standing straight, legs slightly apart, arms hanging down and slightly away from
the body so the hands don't touch the torso. Empty hands, no weapon, no props.

Plain flat light grey background (#E8E8E8), no ground shadow, no floor, no text,
no labels, no frames, no extra characters.
